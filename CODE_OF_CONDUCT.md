# Code of Conduct

fma welcomes people with any background or level of experience. We welcome every
form of PR and changes or additions to any part of the project. AI-assisted and
AI-generated contributions are explicitly welcome and receive the same review as
other work. Tool choice is not a reason to dismiss or harass a contributor.

## Working together

Be respectful and specific. Discuss code, evidence, behavior and tradeoffs rather
than making personal judgments. Disagreement and criticism are welcome when they
help improve the work. Assume questions may come from someone learning the project,
and accept corrections without hostility.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, publishing
private information without consent, and repeated personal attacks are not
acceptable. These conduct expectations apply to everyone, including maintainers,
and cover project issues, PRs, reviews, discussions and other project spaces.

## Contribution policy

The **single-file philosophy** is the project's one non-negotiable architectural
constraint: application Go code belongs in `main.go`, and Go tests belong in
`main_test.go`. Third-party libraries and separate scripts, documentation, templates
and workflows are welcome. Do not use additional or generated source files to
circumvent this constraint. Everything else is open to proposals, modification and
addition. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for practical guidance.

This architectural constraint does not excuse disrespectful behavior. A proposal
that conflicts with it should receive a clear explanation and an opportunity to
revise, not a personal attack. Welcoming all kinds of PR does not imply that every
PR will be merged without review.

## Reporting and moderation

Report a concern to the repository maintainer, [Jabberwocky238](https://github.com/Jabberwocky238),
using an available contact method on their profile. If the concern can be raised
publicly without exposing private information, a repository issue is also an option.
Do not post sensitive personal information in a public report.

Maintainers may ask for a correction, remove abusive content, limit participation,
or ban participants when needed. Responses should be proportionate and explain the
reason where possible. Treat reports with care and share personal details only as
necessary to address the concern. A participant may ask the maintainer to reconsider
an action and provide relevant context.

## 中文说明

欢迎任何背景和经验水平的贡献者，欢迎任何形式的 PR，以及任何部分的修改与补充。明确接受 AI 辅助编程和 AI 生成内容，按相同标准审查；不能因工具选择而贬低或骚扰贡献者。

尊重他人，围绕代码、证据、行为与取舍讨论；接受建设性的批评、分歧和纠正。禁止骚扰、威胁、歧视性辱骂、性骚扰、人肉搜索、未经许可公开私人信息以及反复的人身攻击。维护者同样遵守这些规则。

**单文件哲学是唯一不可破坏的架构约束：运行时 Go 代码在 `main.go`，Go 测试在 `main_test.go`。** 可以使用第三方库，脚本、文档、模板与工作流可以独立存放；不能通过额外源码或生成代码绕过约束。其他所有部分均可提出修改和补充。与架构约束冲突的提案应得到清晰解释和修改机会，不能成为人身攻击的理由。欢迎提交不等于跳过审查自动合并。

发生问题时，可通过维护者 [Jabberwocky238](https://github.com/Jabberwocky238) 个人资料上可用的联系方式反馈；不涉及隐私的问题也可公开提交 issue。不要在公开报告中暴露敏感个人信息。维护者可按情况要求改正、移除不当内容、限制参与或封禁，并尽可能解释原因。处理应与问题程度相称，谨慎保护报告信息；参与者可以提供相关背景并请求重新考虑处理决定。
